# ASO Keyword Research Guide

This guide explains how to research app store keywords with the free ASO keyword tool. Enter your app to instantly see its keywords, rankings, and search data. The tool works for the App Store and Google Play, and no credit card is needed to create a free account.

## What the Free ASO Keyword Tool Shows

The tool shows real keyword data collected from the App Store and Google Play. The data is regularly updated across 2M+ apps in 250 countries and regions.

For each app, you can see the keywords it already ranks for, together with these metrics:

| Metric | What It Means |
| --- | --- |
| Keywords | The search terms this app already ranks for. |
| Ranking | Where your app currently ranks for each keyword. |
| Value Score | A value score modeled from multiple data points. |
| Vol | A search popularity index. Vol is not the exact monthly search volume. |
| Results | The number of competing results for a keyword. |

## Find App Keywords in 3 Simple Steps

### Step 1: Create a free account

Create a free account. No credit card needed.

### Step 2: Choose a store and country, then search for an app

Choose a store and country, then search for an app. You can choose the App Store or Google Play, select a country such as the United States of America, and search for an app by name, link, or ID.

### Step 3: Explore the app's ranked keywords and search data

Explore the app's ranked keywords and search data. The results list shows each keyword with its ranking, value score, Vol, and Results, so you can compare popularity and competition.

## What You Can Do with the Tool

- **Keyword Analysis:** Explore keywords by value, popularity, and competition.
- **Ranking Check:** Check where your app currently ranks for each keyword.
- **Competitor Analysis:** Discover your competitors' keywords and where they rank.

## How to Choose Keywords

Start with keywords that are relevant to your app. Then compare Vol (popularity), Results (competition), and Value Score before choosing the keywords to target in your app title, subtitle, and description.
